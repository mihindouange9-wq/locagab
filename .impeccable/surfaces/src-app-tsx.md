# Surface brief — site vitrine LOCAGAB (page d'accueil)

Scope: the single-page product site of the LOCAGAB mobile app. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: someone looking for housing in Gabon, on a phone, tired of unreliable listings; secondarily owners, agencies, investors and media. Action: download the app (App Store / Google Play). Proof available: the app screens themselves, built in HTML/CSS, and the verification mechanism (Trust Score, availability). No figures, testimonials or partners exist and none are shown.

Constraints: palette, Manrope, section list, copy and the three-iPhone hero are pinned by the client brief of 2026-10-06 and beat any roll. Never a marketplace, never a dominant search bar. No decision round was held with the client: the brief was treated as the answer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the site is where a listing becomes a verified fact. Every section speaks the grammar of proof (a ring-and-check mark, a status, a reference, a date) and refuses the category default of a search bar over a grid of property cards.

OWN-WORLD: warm ivory ground, with one darker sand band for the row of screens (« L'application en action ») so the seven devices read on a ground of their own; deep forest fields that own whole sections (trust, download, footer); white only as the app's own surface; LOCAGAB green only on actions and on the verification mark. One device, the ring taken from the eye of the logo's pin: availability dot, check seal, map marker, step marker. Forest hairlines as form rules, tabular figures for prices and references, uppercase tracked labels for status only.

STORY: this is a phone app for housing in Gabon; its listings are checked and their availability is known; download it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: nav (logo left, six anchors, download button right). Left column: status label, H1 on three lines with « simplement. » in green, one sentence, two actions. Right column: three iPhones on one axis, the centre one larger (home screen), map and listing behind it, each screen live HTML, with two hairline callouts naming what is verified and available.

FORM: candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list, « le dossier vérifié » (the certified record). Seed key 55308eaf. Raises: from the treasure map, the Libreville map is drawn from the real coastline first and labels fit after; from the Versailles axis, the phone trio and the download close each sit on one strict axis (on desktop the hero's pinned two-column first viewport puts the trio's axis in the right column while the close is centred; on mobile both share the page axis); from the depot blind, screen changes inside a phone step whole rows instead of gliding. Signature interaction: the Trust Score assembling, five verifications checked in turn while the figure climbs to 95 %. Motion grammar: slow, exact, no bounce.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
